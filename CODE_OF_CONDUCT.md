# Community Code of Conduct

We want MxTrove to be a welcoming place for musicians and developers with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, assume good intent, explain technical disagreements clearly,
and accept constructive feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse,
sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.
Do not publish another person's private data or content without permission.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces maintained by the MxTrove project. Maintainers may edit or remove
inappropriate content, close discussions, or temporarily/permanently exclude
participants, in proportion to the behavior. They will consider context and
listen to affected people.

Report incidents privately to mxtrove@wlknbrch.com. Include relevant links and
context, and avoid unnecessary personal information. Reports will be handled
with discretion; complete confidentiality cannot be promised when action is
needed. If a maintainer is involved, state that in the report so another
available maintainer can review it.
