# MxTrove name and branding

MxTrove is a project by Tom Maucksch. The project name, logos, and associated
branding identify the original project and its official services. This policy
does not claim that a trademark is registered.

## Forks and independent services

You may fork, modify, redistribute, and host MxTrove under the AGPL. You may
accurately say that your software is "based on MxTrove", "a fork of MxTrove",
or "compatible with MxTrove", and link to the upstream project.

Make the operator and independent status of your service clear. A suitable
notice is: "An independent service based on MxTrove, operated by [operator].
Not affiliated with or endorsed by the MxTrove project."

Use your own service name and logo where existing branding would imply that
your modified service is operated, approved, or supported by the upstream
project. Do not describe an independent service as "official MxTrove", use
misleading account/domain names, or suggest endorsement without permission.
Truthful attribution and unmodified source distributions remain permitted.

Keep copyright and license notices required by the applicable licenses.
Replacing service branding does not require removing upstream attribution.

## Scope

The AGPL governs copyright permissions for the software and project-provided
assets; it does not grant trademark rights. This document addresses misleading
branding and endorsement, not software use, commercial hosting, modification,
or redistribution. It adds no further restriction to the AGPL.
